# Structures - integration notes

All four `.nbt` files are gzip-compressed vanilla structure templates (DataVersion 3955 = Java 1.21.1), generated by script
and read back with an independent parser. **They have not been loaded in a running Minecraft client** (see Verification).

| File | Size X x Y x Z | Align structure layer y= with terrain surface | Creature encounter area |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cathedral_of_Glass_Harp.nbt | 39 x 32 x 39 | 8 | x=19, y=9, z=24..27 (open floor in front of ceremonial chamber, between the two harps) |
| Buried_Mirage_Observatory.nbt | 31 x 28 x 31 | 15 | x=15, y=16, z=15 (star-map hall center) |
| Lost_Caravanserai.nbt | 43 x 19 x 43 | 4 | x=25..30, y=5, z=12..18 (fenced, canopied resting area NE courtyard) |
| Thunderglass_Labyrinth.nbt | 49 x 25 x 49 | 6 | x=24, y=7, z=24, radius 8 (central circular sanctuary around the fractured monument) |

Placement: origin = minimum corner of the template. `origin_y = terrain_surface_y - surface_layer_y`.
Undersized terrain: the Cathedral and Caravanserai have flat floor plates (see worldgen spec for `beard_box`/terrain adaptation).

## Custom blocks / vanilla substitutes
The NBT files use **vanilla blocks only** so they load in any test world. `Custom_Block_Substitution_Map.json` lists which
custom assets the vanilla blocks stand in for. Replace palette entries (not positions) to upgrade a structure to custom blocks.
Block entities: chests carry vanilla `LootTable` references (desert_pyramid, buried_treasure, ancient_city, village_desert_house);
no entities (mobs) are stored - spawn the Prism Jerboa through worldgen rules.

## Structure feature checklist (what was actually built)
**01 Cathedral of Glass Harp** - elliptical vaulted nave, 6 golden-glass arch ribs, 10 sandstone columns, pointed glass windows,
two giant end-rod-string harps, hanging chain+amethyst chimes (15), U-shaped balconies (2 sides + entrance loft, stairs both sides),
ceremonial chamber with altar and chests, geometric floor (concentric purpur/gold/diagonals), hidden crystal vault reached by
breaking the two chiseled sandstone floor blocks at (19,8,31..32) -> stair shaft -> vault (x10..28,z7..26,y2..6).
**02 Buried Mirage Observatory** - circular tower 31x31, sand mound burying the south/east, 3 accessible floors (library y10-14,
star-map hall y16-21, gallery ring y23+), collapsed glass/gold dome, broken glass telescope + fallen section, black/blue star-map floor
with glowstone stars and gold ring, spiral stairs, bookshelf library, secret ladder shaft behind bookshelf at (5,10..13,15) to underground
research room (r=9) and a sealed treasure chamber behind chiseled blocks at (24,2..3,15).
**03 Lost Caravanserai** - fortified south gate with portcullis bars, 4 corner towers (stairs to roof), roof walkways with parapets,
arcade ring around courtyard, 8 furnished merchant rooms (4 west/4 east, doors face the arcade), 2 north warehouses + south storage,
guard room with roof stairs, oasis pond with palms, canopy market (4 awnings), fenced canopy creature rest area, tunnel ring under the
arcade (stairs in north-west warehouse), hidden treasure vault under SW tower behind chiseled blocks at (9,1..3,38).
**04 Thunderglass Labyrinth** - 12x12-cell maze (seed 666) of black/purple stained-glass walls on blackstone bases, 4 entrance towers,
obelisks (8), broken yellow-glass bridges (4), lightning formations (4), amethyst statues (8), central circular sanctuary (r=8) with
12 pillars and a fractured multi-shard crystal monument, 4 hidden 7x3 chambers (secret cracked blackstone-brick blocks), underground
treasure chamber reached by breaking chiseled blackstone at (24,6,30..31).

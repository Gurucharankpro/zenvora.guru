**01. Refai's Legendary Chair** — Dark carved-wood Egyptian armchair, black leather cushions, lion-shaped gold armrests, crimson embroidery, gold trim. 
**02. Refai's Memorial Statue** — Bronze-and-charcoal statue of Refai (bald, thick black beard, sunglasses, dark coat, arms crossed) on a blackstone pedestal. 
**03. Nasser's Executive Desk** — Dark walnut executive desk with drawers, stacked documents, miniature lamp, golden nameplate (ناصر الدسوقي) and an integrated black leather chair. 
**04. The Twin Brothers Portrait** — Wall-mounted ornate golden frame holding a 96x96 pixel-art portrait of Refai (sunglasses, black beard) and Nasser (grey hair, red tie) in dark clothes. Back plane sits on the south face (z=16) so the portrait faces NORTH at default rotation.
**05. Salmawy's Secret Archive Cabinet** — Antique wooden archive cabinet with book shelves, sealed envelopes, mysterious folders, silver handles and a hidden compartment drawer. 
**06. The Black Marble Conference Table** — Rectangular polished black-marble table with ornate gold legs, scattered documents and a central crimson crystal ornament. 
**07. Traditional Egyptian Majlis** — Seating-area decoration: carved wooden bench, patterned cushions and bolsters, ornamental kilim carpet and a low carved side table. 
**08. Antique Cairo Grandfather Clock** — Tall vintage walnut clock with brass hands, Arabic-numeral face, glass trunk panel, geometric Egyptian carvings and swinging-pendulum geometry (static). 
**09. El Nemr's Golden Lion Throne** — Imposing gold-and-burgundy throne: carved wood panels, lion-head armrests, velvet seat and backrest with a large golden lion crest. 
**10. The El Nemr Family Emblem** — Wall-mounted black-marble shield with an original golden tiger-head emblem, engraved silver ornaments and a small gold crest. 
**11. Egyptian Workshop Tool Cabinet** — Weathered steel workshop cabinet with riveted drawers, brass handles, a pegboard with hammer, wrench, saw and decorative gears, and tools lying on top. 
**12. Vintage Workshop Machinery** — Static compact industrial machine on a reinforced steel workbench: bronze flywheel gear, belt, copper pipes, brass pressure gauge, vice and toolbox. 
**13. Egyptian Mobile Phone Display Counter** — Shop counter with glass-like display case, miniature smartphones on stands, colorful display risers, charging cables and a polished wooden base. 
**14. Morsi's Mobile Shop Sign** — Wall/hanging illuminated-looking storefront sign with dark-blue frame and phone icons. Texture text (exact): "مرسي للمحمول" (Morsi lil-Mahmoul, "Morsi Mobile"). Arabic text is baked into sign_ar region (0,0)-(64,24) of the texture, rendered with proper letter joining and RTL order.
**15. Hanan's Traditional Vanity** — Elegant carved wooden dressing table with an oval mirror, golden jewelry boxes, perfume bottles, floral drawers and a small cushioned stool. 
**16. Egyptian Family Tea Set** — Low carved wooden serving table with embroidered cloth, silver tray, brass teapot, sugar bowl and transparent-looking amber tea glasses, plus floor cushions. 
**17. Old Cairo Police Office Desk** — Vintage administrative desk in dark wood: green felt blotter, document folders, rotary telephone, brass lamp, stationery, name plaque (كمال مراد) and an old wooden office chair. 
**18. Egyptian Police Station Wall Clock** — Weathered-brass framed wall clock with Arabic-Indic numerals (١ … ١٢), dark wood backing and geometric border studs. 
**19.
